MOTIONS: move without the arrow keys or the mouse.

  h j k l    left down up right   (j looks like a down arrow)
  w  b  e    next word, back a word, end of word
  0  $       start of line, end of line
  gg  G      top of file, bottom of file
  5j         counts work on everything: move 5 lines down

Try it:
  1. Use only h j k l to land on the X:

       . . . . . . . . . .
       . . . . . . . X . .
       . . . . . . . . . .

  2. Use w and b to hop between the words on this line, then e to ride the ends.

  3. On this line, press 0, then $, then 0 again.

  4. Press G to fly to the bottom, then gg to come back.

  5. From here, type 3j to jump exactly three lines down.

     one
     two
     three  <- you should be here
