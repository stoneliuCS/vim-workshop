THE GRAMMAR: verb + count + noun.

  verbs:   d delete    c change    y yank (copy)    p put (paste)
  nouns:   any motion you already know: w b e 0 $ gg G j k

  dw    delete word            d$    delete to end of line
  c2w   change two words       yy    yank the whole line (doubled = line)
  dd    delete the line        p     put it back below

Try it:
  1. Delete the word "very" with dw:
       Vim is very very fast.

  2. Change "slow" to "fast" with cw:
       My editor is slow.

  3. Delete from "BUT" to the end of the line with d$:
       Keep this part BUT throw away everything here.

  4. Duplicate this line with yy then p.

  5. Delete these three lines at once with 3dd:
       delete me
       delete me
       delete me
