MODES: Normal is home. Everything else is a visit.

  i       go to Insert mode (type text)
  <Esc>   go back home to Normal mode
  v       Visual mode (select stuff)
  :       Command mode (:w save, :q quit, :q! quit without saving)

Try it:
  1. Put your cursor on the line below, press i, type your name, press <Esc>.

     My name is:

  2. Press o to open a new line below this one and type "hi", then <Esc>.

  3. Press A on this line to jump to the end and type " (done)", then <Esc>.

  4. Press u to undo. Press <C-r> to redo. Nothing is ever really lost.

  5. Type :q! and press Enter. Congrats, you can now leave Vim.
